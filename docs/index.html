Melee

Weapon choice is a trade again: reach against damage, swing timer against raw hit, a shield against a free off hand.

Sword blocking

Hold right-click with any sword to raise it, like 1.8. It halves a frontal hit, never erases it. Blocking with a sword is for when the shield is at home.

Shortswords

Iron and diamond only. No swing timer, so every hit lands at full strength however fast you click, but each hit is weaker than the sword of the same metal.

Iron · Diamond

Weapon reach

Tridents reach further, axes swing shorter. Everything else keeps vanilla's 3 blocks.

Trident 3.5Sword 3.0Axe 2.5

Thrown fireballs

Right-click a fire charge to throw it as a Ghast-style fireball. It flies dead straight at constant speed and explodes where it lands.

Speed 3Blast 2Cooldown 0.5 s

Endgame weapons

Three weapons borrowed from Terraria and its Calamity mod, and priced like it: each one takes a nether star.

Exoblade

A big blade: it outreaches every other sword, and a full-strength swing cuts everything in the arc in front of you, not just what you aimed at. Every full swing also throws a homing Exobeam, and whatever a beam hits is cut by a flurry of Exo slashes. Hold attack and it keeps swinging, a full swing each time the meter fills. Right-click to lunge: the first thing you hit takes a heavy blow and you bounce back off it, briefly invulnerable. Swing straight after a lunge lands for the big slash: a huge arc that throws three beams at once. Lunge in, bounce out, swing.

Reach 4.5Blade 14Beam 8Lunge 20Big slash 24

Scarlet Devil

Right-click to throw, and hold it to keep throwing. It never leaves your hand: what flies is a piercing spear of red light that sheds homing bullets and bursts into a Scarlet Blast on every hit. Go two seconds without throwing and it chimes: the next throw is a Gungnir, Calamity's stealth strike. It hits twice as hard, blasts twice as wide and heals you for everything it strikes.

Spear 14Throws every 0.6 sStealth strike Gungnir ×2Never used up

Zenith

Terraria's own last sword, forged from every other one, and it swings none of them. Each full swing sends phantoms of the swords that went into it looping out to whatever you aim at and back to your hand, cutting through everything on the way there and on the way back, walls included. Hold attack and it keeps swinging, so the air in front of you fills with them.

Crafting

Movement

Two ways to get somewhere fast on foot: a dash to close the gap or get out of trouble, and a slide that keeps your sprint and fits under what you'd otherwise have to crawl through.

Dash

Press R to burst a few blocks the way you're walking. Hold A or D to sidestep, S to hop back, or nothing to go where you're looking. It stays flat, so it covers the same ground in the air as on it. You get one in the air per jump, and dashing mid-fall wipes out the fall damage you'd built up so far.

Distance ~5 blocksCooldown 1.5 sIn the air 1×

Slide

Press Sneak while sprinting and you drop flat and keep going, faster than you were running, easing off over a second. Flat means you fit under a one-block gap. Look left or right to curve it. Let go of sneak to stand up, or jump to launch out of it with the speed it still had.

Start 2.5× sprintUp to 1 sCooldown 0.5 s

Flight

The elytra, rebuilt as an aircraft: real roll and pitch, a throttle, a bomb bay, and a helmet for ramming.

Free-look flight

The mouse steers around the glider's own axes, like a flight sim. W/S pitch, A/D roll. There's no ±90° limit, so loops and barrel rolls work and the controls still match the screen upside down.

Roll 200°/sPitch 120°/s

Rocket boost

Hold Shift while gliding and rockets burn straight out of your inventory for a continuous push. No more hold-and-click.

1 rocket / second

Elytra chestplate

Combine any chestplate with an elytra to get wings without giving up armour. Enchantments, durability and trim carry over. Heavier plate costs you speed.

Gold −5%Most −10%Diamond · Netherite −20%

Elytra bombing

Right-click TNT while gliding and it drops as a bomb carrying your momentum. It arcs forward and slows the way a real bomb does. On the ground, TNT places as normal.

Fuse 3 sCooldown 0.5 s

Battering Ram

A helmet enchantment that makes you the hammer. Fly into a mob fast enough and the damage scales with your speed, with a wind burst and a blast on impact. Walls stop hurting you; the helmet pays in durability instead. Losing your charge leaves you dazed for a moment. Enchanted helmets are drawn permanently dented flat on top.

Min speed 0.8 b/tDamage 4 × speedWind burst 3 blocksLevels I–III

The Gipfäli arsenal

One pastry, five ways to deliver it. It fills you up at breakfast and it's the ammunition for everything below.

Gipfäli

Wheat, wheat, egg, sugar: two croissants. Eat one or load one.

Gipfäli Launcher

Sneak + right-click to lock on to whatever is nearest the middle of your view. The sight zooms in and the target glows through walls. Fire, and the Gipfäli homes in with a real turning circle, so a hard dodge can still beat it. It steers around terrain on the way.

Artillery. Right-click to call a block, right-click again to set the bomb down. It counts five seconds out loud, then lobs itself over and lands exactly on the called block, straight through any hill in the way.

Crafting

The Gipfäli Army

Raise a squad under a commander, kit it out of your own pack, and point. They follow, hold formation, stand on parade, guard what you tell them to, and lead their shots.

Command Flag

The one item the army runs from. Right-click the air to raise a new squad: its commander appears. Click the commander to fill the ranks: +1, +5, +10 or all 40. Right-click a target to send the squad after it, or one of your own soldiers to have it hold there or fall back in. Sneak + right-click opens the army menu: orders, formations, colours, and everything on the map worth attacking.

Squad 40 + commanderArmy 200Recruit 2 GipfäliMarch 192 blocks

Squads & commanders

A squad is a colour: paint a soldier blue and it's in the blue squad. Sixteen dye colours, plus camouflage for the reserve, each uniform with the army's red-and-white armband. Promote one soldier per squad to commander: it changes into a black dress uniform with the squad colour on lapels, cap and stripes. Click it for the whole squad's orders.

Kit & armour

Recruits come unarmed. Click a soldier for its own menu: kit, armour, colour, rank, posts. The gun in its hand is its role, so hand it a different one and it changes role on the spot. Armour is vanilla armour, leather to netherite, and it protects like it does on you. The commander's menu kits out the whole squad at once. Kit and armour come out of your pack. Or skip the menu and right-click a soldier with a gun, a helmet or a dye; a Gipfäli heals it.

Parade

Stand puts the army on parade where you're standing, facing the way you face: each squad a block of ranks, commanders out in front. They stand at attention and fire at nothing unless fired on. Stand follow marches the same ranks after you, and Attack sends them back into the field.

Block 5 × 8

Guard Post

Set it down wherever there's something to defend, click it, and station soldiers there. Posts are numbered as you place them, and a soldier's own menu can send it to one to walk a beat. You set how wide they watch and how touchy they are: Defend takes on monsters, Aggressive strangers too, Passive only what hits them. Break the post and the guard is gone.

Recoil & headshots

Guns climb and shove, and the climb moves your real aim, so it costs you the next shot. Soldiers lead moving targets. Headshots hit double.

Headshot ×2

Field manual & commands

Your first recruit comes with a written field manual, and the army menu hands you another. Every order is also a command: /gipfaeliarmy. Operators get two more: parade raises a parade ground of soldiers out of nothing, and fortress throws up stone-brick walls around you, a tower on each corner, a gate, and a guard post at every corner and the gate with its guards already stationed.

Rifleman. A three-round burst and decent reach. The standard.

Assault. A fistful of crumbs at once, and nothing at all past a dozen blocks. Walks right in and takes a beating.

Marksman Rifle

Marksman. One crumb, hard, from further off than anything else here. Hangs back.

Gipfäli Launcher

Bomber. Homing Gipfäli, the same launcher you carry. Won't fire point blank.

Heavy MG

Panzer. Off a tank mount. Endless fire, and the armour and weight to match.

Marcher. No gun. Everyone within ten blocks of it is faster, hits harder and takes more.

Formations

Worked out fresh from where you stand and which way you face, so the shape walks and turns with you. A fight breaks formation; they re-form after. Parade is the one that stays put: each squad its own block of ranks, side by side.

Territory

Claim the ground you build on, chunk by chunk. Hold it, or watch someone stand on it long enough to take it.

Claim

Press M for a map of the chunks around you with every claim coloured in. Claiming the chunk you stand in is free and instant.

Limit 8 chunks= 2,048 blocks

Protect

Inside your land nobody else breaks or places blocks, opens your chests, doors or switches, or blows anything up.

Capture

Stand in someone else's chunk to take it. The owner gets your name and the chunk. Leave, die or log out and the capture lapses. Players who are offline get the news when they next log in.

Capture 30 s

See the borders

A low wall of dust marks every claim edge near you: green for yours, red for theirs, orange while a chunk is being taken. Send your army to march on a chunk straight from the map.

Extras

Floating damage numbers, a training dummy to read them off, a config screen with a switch for everything, and a block of ham.

Damage numbers

Every hit you land floats its number up beside whatever you hit. Every player nearby sees it, and nothing extra is needed on the client. Want to read them in peace? That's what the dummy below is for.

Placeable · stacks to 16

Training Dummy

A sack of straw on a post, arms out like a scarecrow. Set it down, try a weapon on it, and read what every hit did off the damage numbers. Hit it and it wobbles on its post the way an armour stand does.

Never dies
Every hit goes through armour, enchantments and damage events exactly as it would on a mob. Only then is its health put back, so the number you see is what that hit really did. Only /kill and the void remove it.
Never moves
Not for knockback, explosions, water, the Battering Ram or the Exoblade's lunge. It can still fall, so it never hangs in the air over a mined-out block.
Counts as a monster
So Exobeams, Scarlet bullets, the launcher's lock and the army's target menu all go after it. Iron golems are kept off it, so one in a village isn't punched forever.
Dress it
Right-click it with armour to put that piece on, and get back whatever it replaced. Try the same weapon against leather, then netherite. Sneak + right-click with an empty hand picks it up, armour and all.

Install

Minecraft Java 26.3 with NeoForge. Needed on the server and on every client that joins.

  1. Get NeoForge 26.3.0.7-betaRun the installer from neoforged.net and pick Install client. It adds a NeoForge profile to the launcher.
  2. Add the modDrop combatupdate-1.0.0.jar into .minecraft/mods. Servers take it in their own mods folder.
  3. Launch the NeoForge profileCombatUpdate shows up under Mods, with its config one click away.
  4. Or build it yourselfClone the repo and run ./gradlew build. The jar lands in build/libs.
